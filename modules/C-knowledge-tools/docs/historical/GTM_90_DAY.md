# Praxis Ledger 90-Day Go-to-Market Plan

## Days 0-30: Problem Proof

- Interview 12 managers, 5 executive coaches, and 5 L&D/enablement buyers.
- Secure five design partners; at least two must be organizations, not friends/individuals.
- Run the MVP with sample data before asking for diary imports or integrations.
- Test three entry moments: difficult conversation, priority/resource decision, and customer/commitment decision.
- Charge for at least one design-partner pilot; free interest is not payment evidence.

Exit criteria:

- 60% invited-user activation.
- Median first loop under 8 minutes.
- At least 40% of users say the structured artifact is meaningfully different from a normal journal or AI chat.
- One paid pilot or three signed letters of intent with a price and start date.

## Days 31-60: Retention and Trust Proof

- Run three cohorts with 75-150 total managers.
- Add outcome reminders, evidence-linked patterns, export/delete, and explicit sharing.
- Deliver only aggregate activation/closure metrics to buyers.
- Validate security architecture with two customer IT/privacy reviewers.
- Recruit 20 coaches to a partner waitlist and activate 5 with clients.

Exit criteria:

- 35%+ week-4 retention among activated users.
- 45%+ loop closure.
- 25%+ accepted-cue reuse.
- No request from a credible design partner to expose raw notes to admins.

## Days 61-90: Commercial Proof

- Convert two pilots to annual contracts.
- Start five paid pilots at or near US$15,000.
- Publish one permissioned case study using process/outcome metrics, not private content.
- Create coach certification/workshop material for the decision-loop method.
- Build the minimum SSO, DPA, retention, region, and audit-log package required by signed customers.

Exit criteria:

- At least US$100,000 contracted ARR or signed annual value.
- 50%+ paid-pilot conversion among completed pilots.
- A repeatable buyer/champion combination and one repeatable channel.

## Messaging Tests

1. **“Close the loop on consequential decisions.”**
2. **“Your private decision memory, not another AI coach.”**
3. **“See the evidence behind your patterns.”**
4. **“Leadership learning without employee surveillance.”**

## Initial Channels

- Executive-coach and leadership-facilitator partners.
- Existing manager-development cohorts.
- Product, customer-success, sales, and operations leadership communities.
- A self-serve individual edition that exports a coach-ready decision brief.

Paid acquisition is deferred until activation and week-4 retention meet thresholds.
