# Praxis Ledger Business Model

## 1. Customer and Buyer

Primary ideal customer profile:

- 200-3,000 employee knowledge-work organizations.
- Existing manager development, executive coaching, sales leadership, or product leadership programs.
- 50-500 managers who make frequent people, priority, customer, or resource decisions.
- Buyer: VP People/L&D, Chief People Officer, leadership-program owner, or function enablement leader.
- Champion: executive coach, leadership facilitator, or manager enablement lead.

Initial end-user segment:

- New and mid-level managers with 5-15 direct/indirect reports.
- Product, customer success, sales, and operations leaders with frequent ambiguous decisions.
- Human coaches who need structured between-session evidence without reading a client's full diary.

## 2. Positioning

**Category:** private decision memory / decision debrief system.

**Not:** therapy, mental-health analytics, personality assessment, employee surveillance, spiritual measurement, or generic AI coaching.

**Promise:** “Close the loop on consequential decisions.”

The product helps a user create a pre/post decision record, compare prediction with outcome, identify repeated evidence-backed patterns, and carry an if-then cue into the next similar event.

## 6. North-Star and Funnel Metrics

North-star metric:

**Closed decision loops per weekly active user**: records with an initial prediction/action and a later actual outcome/review.

Activation:

- 60% of invited users create a first event within 7 days.
- Median time to first useful record under 8 minutes.
- 50% schedule or complete an outcome follow-up.

Retention/value:

- 35%+ week-4 retained among activated users during pilots.
- 45%+ of created loops receive an outcome review.
- 25%+ of accepted if-then cues are referenced in a later similar event.
- 30%+ of active users confirm at least one repeated pattern with two or more supporting events by day 45.

Enterprise trust:

- Zero raw-note views by organization admins.
- 100% of shared records have an explicit share action and visible audience.
- Deletion/export requests complete within the published SLA.

Commercial:

- 50%+ paid-pilot-to-annual conversion.
- Sales cycle under 90 days for Business and under 180 days for Enterprise.
- 110%+ net revenue retention after the first annual cohort.

## 7. Defensibility

The moat is not the LLM or the book text.

1. **Outcome-labeled decision graph:** longitudinal relationships between situations, interpretations, actions, predictions, and actual outcomes.
2. **User-confirmed pattern layer:** every pattern has evidence and explicit acceptance/rejection, producing higher-quality labels than passive sentiment analytics.
3. **Trust architecture:** private raw records, granular sharing, no workplace emotion recognition, and content-free analytics.
4. **Workflow distribution:** coach and leadership-program templates create repeatable acquisition and domain-specific decision loops.
5. **Evaluation corpus:** de-identified, consented structural metrics can improve prompts and deterministic heuristics without retaining raw private text.

## 8. Principal Risks and Kill Criteria

| Risk | Early test | Kill/redirect threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Users will not record difficult decisions | 5 design partners, 100 invited managers | <30% activation after two onboarding iterations |
| Outcome follow-up feels like work | Reminder and one-tap review experiments | <25% loop closure after two iterations |
| Buyers see it as redundant with coaching suites | Buyer interviews and paid pilot proposals | No paid pilot from 20 qualified buyers |
| Privacy concerns block deployment | Security/privacy review with design partners | Required employer raw-note access or passive monitoring |
| No measurable value | Prediction calibration, cue reuse, decision follow-through | No improvement signal after 3 pilots / 12 weeks |
| Coaches reject AI involvement | Coach edition prototype | <3 of 10 coaches willing to run a paid client pilot |

If the privacy model must be weakened to close sales, the enterprise direction should be rejected rather than turning the app into surveillance.
